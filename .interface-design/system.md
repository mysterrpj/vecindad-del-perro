# Dashboard system

## Direction

Compact operational workspace for staff managing pets, bookings, prices, and payments. Use calm graphite surfaces, violet for selection and primary actions, and green for availability.

## Navigation

- Inicio: Hoy and Resumen tabs.
- Reservas.
- Clientes: Clientes, Mensajes, and Reclamos tabs.
- Finanzas: Pagos and Reportes tabs.
- Servicios.
- Configuracion.

Keep the sidebar at 232px. Do not add primary links for views that already belong to an internal tab group.

## Visual rules

- Borders-only depth on dark graphite surfaces.
- Spacing base: 4px; common values: 8px, 12px, 16px, 20px, 28px.
- Controls use 10px radius, panels 12px, and modals 14px.
- Service catalog: dense rows with search, filters, and a modal editor. Lock the ID during editing and confirm new IDs.
